## 3. Known issues in the source data

- **Answer offsets.** IndicQA's `answer_start` often does not point at the answer text. The loader realigns offsets and records how (DECISIONS D-017). Questions whose offset can't be recovered are kept, but their answer span is not used.
- **Answers missing from their passage** (*not found* above). In a few languages some answer texts do not occur in their passage at all. Checking those in Malayalam, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi and Telugu: almost all are genuinely different text (one even starts with a stray `.`). Only a handful differ just in whitespace; NFC normalisation does not help. Revisit before enabling a language in M1.
- **Repeated questions.** A few questions recur word for word across articles. They are kept in one split (DECISIONS D-018).
- **Unanswerable questions** (category `NO`) are kept and flagged. They are useful for testing whether the generator admits that the evidence has no answer.
