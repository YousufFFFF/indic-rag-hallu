## 3. Restated research gap

There is no RAG-grounded, span-annotated hallucination resource or detector for **multiple** Indic languages:

- the span-level multilingual sets (PsiloQA, Mu-SHROOM) are not RAG, and Hindi is their only Indic language;
- the Indic hallucination benchmark (BHRAM-IL) is question-level and not grounded in retrieved evidence;
- the Indic RAG resource (IndicRAGSuite) has no hallucination labels;
- the RAG span detectors (LettuceDetect v1/v2 and their adaptations) have no Indic training or evaluation.
